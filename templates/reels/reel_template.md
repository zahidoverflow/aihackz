## Structure (Optimal Duration: 25–45 seconds)

### 1. The 3-Second Visual & Verbal Hook (0:00 - 0:03)
*Goal: Stop the scroll immediately.*
- **Verbal Examples:**
  - *"Stop wasting 3 hours on [Task]. Do this AI hack instead..."*
  - *"99% of people have no idea their phone can do this..."*
  - *"This single Termux command turns your Android into an autonomous AI bot..."*
  - *"Don't pay for [Software]. Here's the free AI alternative..."*
- **Visual:** Fast zoom-in on mobile screen / code terminal with bold highlighted text.

### 2. The Problem & Pain Point (0:03 - 0:08)
- Describe the frustrating manual process or expensive paywall.

### 3. The "Hack" Demonstration (0:08 - 0:25)
- Step 1: Open the tool / command / workflow.
- Step 2: Input prompt or execute command.
- Step 3: Show the instant result.

### 4. The Call-To-Action (CTA) & Monetization Funnel (0:25 - 0:35)
*Goal: Convert viewer into subscriber / buyer / community member.*

- *"Read the complete step-by-step breakdown at aihackz.tech."*
